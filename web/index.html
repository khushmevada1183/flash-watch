⚠️ Error 2: Watch Connection Failed (Device Busy)
In Bluefy on iOS, Error 2 (CBErrorConnectionFailed) means the FLASH watch rejected the connection. Smartwatches only accept one active connection at a time.
1
Disconnect other devices: Is your PC, laptop, or another phone currently connected to the watch? Turn off Bluetooth on those devices.
2
Forget in iPhone Settings: Open iPhone Settings → Bluetooth. If FLASH appears in "MY DEVICES", tap (i) → Forget This Device.
3
Force-close the boAt app: Swipe away the boAt Wearables app if it is running in background.
4
Reboot the watch: Hold the physical button on the watch to restart it, then tap Connect Watch again below.
🦁 How to Fix "Web Bluetooth API globally disabled" in Brave:
Brave blocks Web Bluetooth by default for fingerprint protection. You can turn it on in 15 seconds:
1
Click here:
2
Paste into address bar: brave://flags/#brave-web-bluetooth-api and hit Enter.
3
Change Web Bluetooth API to Enabled and click Relaunch.
💡 Or simply open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge where it works immediately!

📱 How to Connect on Android & iOS Detecting…
🍏 Bluefy (iOS) Connection Checklist:
1
Bluetooth Permission: In iPhone Settings → Bluefy, ensure Bluetooth Sharing is turned ON.
2
Do NOT pair in iPhone Bluetooth: If FLASH is listed in iOS Bluetooth Settings, tap Forget This Device. Bluefy connects directly inside the page.
3
Avoid Multi-Connection: If your laptop or another phone is connected to the watch, turn off their Bluetooth. The watch only accepts one connection!
🚀 Open in Bluefy App 📲 Get Bluefy on App Store
🤖 3 Steps for Android (Chrome / Samsung / Edge):
1
Close the official boAt app: Force-stop or close the boAt Wearables app. If the app holds the connection, the browser cannot find the watch.
2
Unpair in Phone Settings: Go to Settings → Bluetooth. If FLASH is paired, tap Unpair / Forget.
3
Turn ON Bluetooth & Location: Android requires Location / Nearby Devices permission for BLE scanning. Open in Google Chrome and tap Connect Watch.
🦁 Fixing Brave Browser "Globally Disabled" Error:

Brave blocks Web Bluetooth out-of-the-box on desktop and mobile. To enable:

1
Copy flag URL:
2
Paste into address bar: brave://flags/#brave-web-bluetooth-api
3
Set Web Bluetooth API to Enabled, then click Relaunch.
Or open in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge where it works immediately.
