⚠️ Error 2: Why Connection Failed (Even if Bluetooth is ON)
In Bluefy on iOS, Error 2 (CBErrorConnectionFailed) occurs at the hardware/iOS level. Even when the watch screen looks idle and no app is running, 3 common hidden blocks cause this:
1
Watch BLE Radio Locked (Single-Connection Limit): The boAt FLASH chip only supports 1 connection. If an earlier scan or session was interrupted, the watch's internal radio remains stuck in "busy" mode until you reboot.
👉 Fix: Long-press the physical crown button on the watch for 3 seconds → tap Restart (or Power Off, then hold to power back on).
2
iPhone Settings "MY DEVICES" Hijack: If FLASH appears in iPhone Settings → Bluetooth (even if it says "Not Connected"), iOS locks the background GATT handle and blocks Bluefy.
👉 Fix: Open iPhone Settings → Bluetooth. Tap (i) next to FLASH → Forget This Device.
3
Control Center vs Real Bluetooth Reset: Toggling Bluetooth from the iPhone swipe-down Control Center does NOT reset the Bluetooth system daemon!
👉 Fix: Open the iPhone Settings app → Bluetooth → toggle the switch OFF → wait 5 seconds → toggle ON.
4
Background boAt App: If the official boAt Wearables or Da Fit app is installed on your phone, swipe it away in the iPhone App Switcher.

📱 How to Connect on Android & iOS Detecting…
🍏 Bluefy (iOS) Connection Checklist:
1
Bluetooth Permission: In iPhone Settings → Bluefy, ensure Bluetooth Sharing is turned ON.
2
Do NOT pair in iPhone Bluetooth: If FLASH is listed in iOS Bluetooth Settings, tap Forget This Device. Bluefy connects directly inside the page.
3
Avoid Multi-Connection: If your laptop or another phone is connected to the watch, turn off their Bluetooth. The watch only accepts one connection!
🚀 Open in Bluefy App 📲 Get Bluefy on App Store
🤖 3 Steps for Android (Chrome / Samsung / Edge):
1
Close the official boAt app: Force-stop or close the boAt Wearables app. If the app holds the connection, the browser cannot find the watch.
2
Unpair in Phone Settings: Go to Settings → Bluetooth. If FLASH is paired, tap Unpair / Forget.
3
Turn ON Bluetooth & Location: Android requires Location / Nearby Devices permission for BLE scanning. Open in Google Chrome and tap Connect Watch.
